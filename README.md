# listener-taste-growth-profile

Research on how listeners' music taste grows and changes as they age — the relationship between
age and music preference, and how that relationship evolves across a listener's life.

## Contents

- [`paper/draft.md`](paper/draft.md) — the paper draft (working title: *"Taste as It Ages: Age,
  Openness, and the Evolution of Music Preference Across the Listening Life"*).
- [`research-notes.md`](research-notes.md) — open questions, hypotheses, and a running log of
  sources still to review.

## Status

**v3.0 — complete, with genre/artist-level age data.** Literature-review-driven, theoretical piece
(no primary data collection yet), 10 sections: introduction, reminiscence bump, discovery-decline
behavior, the MUSIC personality model, an illustrative age-range listening trajectory, a
population-level genre/artist age-demographics survey, a reconciliation of the timing and content
axes, a proposed empirical extension, limitations, and a conclusion. See `research-notes.md` for
what's confirmed vs. still open (a couple of source-verification items remain, plus an explicit
caveat that §6's industry/survey data is directional, not peer-reviewed) — the paper itself is
finished as a synthesis piece; the empirical extension (listening-history-by-age-cohort analysis) is
proposed future work, not started.
